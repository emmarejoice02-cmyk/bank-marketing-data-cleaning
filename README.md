
# Bank Marketing Data Cleaning

## Project Overview

This project focuses on the cleaning, preparation, and organization of a bank marketing dataset for further analysis and business intelligence.

The dataset contains information related to customers who were contacted during a bank marketing campaign. The objective of the data-cleaning process is to transform the raw data into a cleaner, more consistent, and analysis-ready format.

This project demonstrates practical data-cleaning and data-preparation skills using spreadsheet and CSV-based datasets.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Inspect the raw bank marketing data.
- Identify missing, inconsistent, or incorrectly formatted values.
- Clean and standardize the dataset.
- Improve data consistency and usability.
- Prepare the data for analysis and visualization.
- Produce organized datasets that can be used for business insights.
- Demonstrate a practical data-cleaning workflow.

## Dataset

The project contains exported CSV and Excel files generated during the data preparation process.


The dataset is related to a bank marketing campaign and includes customer and campaign-related information that can be used to understand marketing outcomes.

Typical variables in a bank marketing dataset may include:

- Customer age
- Job/occupation
- Marital status
- Education
- Account or financial information
- Contact method
- Campaign duration
- Number of contacts
- Previous campaign outcome
- Marketing campaign response

## Data Cleaning Process


The cleaning workflow involved examining the available datasets and preparing them for analysis.

Key steps include:

1. **Data inspection**  
   Examining the structure, columns, records, and data types.

2. **Missing-value assessment**  
   Checking for blank, null, or incomplete records.

3. **Duplicate checking**  
   Identifying possible duplicate records that could affect analysis.

4. **Data consistency**  
   Standardizing inconsistent entries and 

formats.

5. **Data type checking**  
   Ensuring numerical, categorical, and date-related fields are appropriately represented.

6. **Data organization**  
   Structuring the cleaned information into usable CSV and Excel formats.

7. **Quality checking**  
   Reviewing the cleaned dataset to ensure that the resulting data is suitable for analysis.

## Business Relevance

Clean and reliable customer data is important for financial institutions because it supports better decision-making.

A properly prepared bank marketing dataset can help analysts investigate questions such as:

- Which customer groups are more likely to respond to marketing campaigns?
- Which communication channels perform better?
- How do customer characteristics relate to campaign outcomes?
- How can banks improve future marketing campaigns?
